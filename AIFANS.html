AI Fans

For your safety





        
        My project is designed to increase road safety and improve driving conditions during winter by introducing an intelligent fan system. This system consists of special fans installed in the front grille of the car. When activated, the fans not only blow air but also spray a specially prepared urea solution. Due to its chemical properties, the urea solution melts ice quickly without harming the environment. Compared to traditional salting methods, this approach is both more eco-friendly and less damaging to vehicle surfaces. The main advantage of the system is that it is equipped with smart control. Sensors detect whether there is ice on the road surface and activate the fans only when necessary. Temperature, humidity, and road friction data are analyzed through artificial intelligence algorithms. This allows the system to use the urea solution in an optimized way, preventing waste.
        
        
       The fans are not limited to operating only in front of the wheels, but can also dynamically direct the airflow. As the vehicle moves, the combination of warm air from the fans and the sprayed solution melts the ice efficiently, minimizing the risk of skidding. This system can be especially useful in mountainous regions and harsh winter climates. Another important aspect of the project is its environmental sustainability. Urea is already widely used in diesel engines to reduce emissions, so the automotive industry is familiar with this substance. In my project, however, it is applied in a new way to enhance road safety. This represents both a creative use of existing technology and a step toward developing new eco-friendly solutions. In conclusion, my fan system is beneficial from both technical and social perspectives: it increases safety for drivers, reduces the risk of accidents, offers an alternative to costly winter road maintenance methods, and most importantly, protects the environment.
